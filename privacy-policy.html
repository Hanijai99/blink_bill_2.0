Privacy Policy — Blink Bill

Last Updated: August 22, 2026

Blink Bill ("we", "our", "the app") is a billing and business management application built for shop owners and small businesses in India. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

Blink Bill is designed as a local-first app: your day-to-day business data stays on your own device. The information handled by the app includes:

  • Account Credentials: When you register, your username and shop details are stored along with your password/PIN only as a one-way SHA-256 hash. We never store or see your actual password or PIN.
  • Shop & Business Details: Shop name, address, mobile number, and settings you enter to personalize your billing.
  • Billing Data: Products, prices, quantities, discounts, GST/tax details, payment mode (cash/online), and generated bills.
  • Customer Information: Customer names, contact numbers, and purchase history that you choose to save for your own record-keeping.
  • Stock & Inventory Data: Product details, barcodes, and QR/barcode codes you generate for inventory management.
  • Expense Records: Business expenses you log within the app.
  • UPI Payment Details: UPI IDs and optional bank labels you configure, plus an optional QR image you may upload — stored locally to generate payment QR codes inside the app. We do not process, store, or have access to your actual money transactions — payments happen directly through your chosen UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.).
  • Camera Access: Used only to scan product barcodes. Camera footage is processed live in memory and is not stored or transmitted.

2. How We Store Your Data

Your data is stored at three levels, all designed to keep you in control:

  • On-Device (Primary): Your billing records, inventory, customer history, expenses, and UPI settings are stored locally on your device using browser storage (localStorage). The app works fully offline for these features.
  • On-Device Archive (IndexedDB): To keep the app fast, bills older than 2 months are automatically moved from active storage to a local archive database (IndexedDB) on the same device. Archived bills remain visible in History and Dashboard, and remain included in backups. Nothing leaves your device during archiving.
  • Cloud (Login Accounts Only): Your login account record — username, hashed password/PIN, and shop name/address/mobile — is stored securely in Google Firebase (Firestore) so you can sign in across sessions. Your bills, inventory, customers, and expenses are not uploaded to the cloud.

3. Backup & Restore

  • The Excel Backup feature lets you export all of your data (Inventory, Bills including archived bills, and Expenses) as a spreadsheet file directly to your device.
  • You choose when to back up and where to keep the file. Backup files are never uploaded by us — you are responsible for storing them securely.
  • Restoring a backup replaces the data currently on your device; it does not affect any other user.

4. Third-Party Services

Blink Bill integrates with the following third-party services to provide core functionality:

  • Google Firebase: Used solely to store login account records (username, password hash, shop contact details) described in Section 2, governed by Google's privacy policy.
  • WhatsApp: When you use "Share via WhatsApp", bill details are passed to WhatsApp's own platform, subject to WhatsApp's privacy policy.
  • UPI Apps: Payment QR codes generated inside Blink Bill open in your chosen UPI payment app, subject to their respective privacy policies.
  • Code Libraries (CDNs): The app loads standard code libraries (e.g., charting, scanning, PDF/Excel tools) from public content delivery networks such as jsDelivr and cdnjs. These requests deliver code only — your business data is not sent to them.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the data handled within the app:

  • Passwords and PINs are never stored in readable form — only SHA-256 hashes are kept.
  • Business data stays on your device unless you explicitly export it yourself.
  • We recommend keeping your device locked, backing up regularly via the Excel Backup feature, and not sharing your login with unauthorized persons.

6. Notifications

If you allow notification permission (where available), Blink Bill may use it only for useful business alerts — for example low-stock reminders. You can turn notifications off anytime in your browser or device settings. We do not send advertising or promotional notifications.

7. Your Rights & Control

  • You can view, edit, or delete your business, product, and customer data at any time within the app.
  • You can export a full backup (including archived bills) or restore data whenever you wish.
  • You can uninstall the app at any time, which removes locally stored data depending on your device settings.
  • To request deletion of your cloud login record, contact us using the details below.

8. Children's Privacy

Blink Bill is intended for business use by shop owners and is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our app or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
